# 3Knights — S.A.C. Assembly creative studies

Three independent design directions for the S.A.C. Official Website: Forest × Coral, Desert × Cobalt and Neutral Studio. Includes interactive Material Library and floating quick navigation.

**Public design study / prototype, not the official company website.** All material swatches, technical visuals, finish names and 3D/architectural renderings are conceptual and must not be interpreted as verified product specifications or real manufacturing installations. Images from third-party references may require separate permission for commercial use. Replace them with approved imagery before promoting as an official company site. The S.A.C. Product Studio is a separate deployment.

## Pages
- `index.html` redirects to `compare.html` (all creative directions)
- `forest.html`, `desert.html`, `neutral.html`: creative directions
- `materials.html?direction=forest|desert|neutral`: interactive material sample book

## Development
All pages are static HTML/CSS/JS; no build step. Assets live in `assets/`. Changes to the official website and Product Studio are out of scope for this repository.
